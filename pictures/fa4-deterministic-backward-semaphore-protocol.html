Device/GPU scope is required. Release publishes completed gradient writes; acquire makes them visible before the next owner starts. The chain repeats transitively for every rank.
What it is — and is not

Ticket protocol, not CAS contention

FA4 protocol
Wait for an exact predetermined rank, reduce, drain, then increment. Arithmetic order is fixed across launches.
CAS mutex
CAS(free → locked) gives ownership to whichever CTA wins the race. Mutual exclusion alone does not make floating-point order deterministic.
Relaxed only
Keeps the counter atomic, but does not publish gradient writes at another address. A successor could observe the ticket without the required data visibility.
Equivalent option
Relaxed atomics plus correctly paired device-scope release/acquire fences can work—but that reconstructs the same ordering semantics explicitly.
