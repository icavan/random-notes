Determinism is orthogonal to the transport primitive

Both bulk reduce and red.global can produce run-to-run FP32 order variation when multiple CTAs update the same location. FA4 adds an explicit ownership chain:

prior data writes→release increment⇢acquire exact rank→next reduction

DSA B200 evidence

Even for contiguous row runs, bulk reduce was only ~2.4% faster at 2 rows; 1/4/8 rows were near parity and 16 rows were slower. That primitive-level margin cannot pay for real-kernel SMEM remap, barriers, and sparse-row issue overhead.
